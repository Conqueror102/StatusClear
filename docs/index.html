Why your Status gets blurry

We posted test clips at different quality levels and measured what WhatsApp did to each. Status keeps anything under about 1.4 Mbps untouched. Anything above gets crushed to 360×640. StatusClear keeps every clip under 1.3 Mbps.

Everything it does for you

Pick a video or photo, tap once, share. The tricky parts happen in the background.

Stays under the limit

Every clip is checked after it's made. If one comes out too big, it's redone smaller automatically.

Better encoder

Uses x264, a professional video encoder that looks much sharper at small sizes than your phone's built-in one.

Full-HD portrait

Made for the tall Status screen. Wide pictures get "Whole picture" or "Fill screen", whichever you prefer.

Long videos, split

Videos are cut into 30-second clips, in order. 60 fps and HDR videos are handled too.

Sharp photos

Photos and screenshots become a crisp 5-second clip. "Zoom into text" helps with small writing.

Private

Everything happens on your phone. No account, no uploads, no ads, no tracking.

Install in a minute

StatusClear isn't on the Play Store yet, so you install the file directly. That's normal for open-source apps.

Download

Tap Download for Android on your phone.

Allow the install

Open the file. Android asks to allow installs from your browser — allow it, then tap Install.

Post

Pick a video or photo, tap Make HD Status, then Share to WhatsApp → My status.

Which download? Almost every phone from the last 7+ years needs the standard version (arm64). If it says "app not installed" on an older phone, use the 32-bit version (armeabi-v7a). All versions are on the releases page.

Questions

Is it safe?

Yes. The full source code is public on GitHub, so anyone can check exactly what it does. It needs no special permissions, has no ads or trackers, and never uploads your files.

Why does Android warn me when installing?

Android shows that warning for any app installed from outside the Play Store. Each release is signed with the same key, so updates install cleanly over the old version.

Does it work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes. If WhatsApp isn't installed but WhatsApp Business is, StatusClear shares straight to it.

My Status still looks soft — why?

Don't trim, crop or add text in WhatsApp after sharing: any edit makes WhatsApp re-encode it. For wide pictures, try "Fill screen" so the picture uses the whole Status screen.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet — StatusClear is Android only for now.
